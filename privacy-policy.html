Privacy Policy

Haunted Space · Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Haunted Space ("the game", "we", "us") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer.

Summary

  • You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device information as described below.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (for example unlocked items, currencies, settings, purchases you own and Daily Climb progress) in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • how you interact with the ads.

Google may use this information to show personalised ads, depending on your settings and your region. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment information under its own privacy policy. The game only receives confirmation of what you bought, so it can unlock the item on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run our own servers that collect your data, and the game has no analytics of its own.
  • The game does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because the game itself keeps your data only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it. For data collected by Google, use the Google links above. You can also contact us with any request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
